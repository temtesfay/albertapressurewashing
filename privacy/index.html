Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alberta Pressure Washing LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit albertapressurewashing.ca or request our services in Alberta, Canada.

Note for the site owner: Replace the bracketed placeholders below (e.g. [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]) with your real details, and have this policy reviewed by a legal professional before relying on it.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address

Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools (for example, Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as scheduling, communications, or website hosting), and when required by law.

We keep your information only as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, and meet legal requirements.

6. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable Alberta legislation, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Our services are directed to property owners and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
