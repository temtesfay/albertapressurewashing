Frequently Asked Questions

What is pressure washing used for?

Pressure washing uses pressurized water to remove dirt, mud, algae, moss, mildew, road salt, and stubborn stains from exterior surfaces — driveways, siding, decks, fences, roofs, gutters, patios, and garage pads. It restores curb appeal and protects your surfaces from long-term damage.

What are the benefits of pressure washing?

Beyond a cleaner look, regular pressure washing protects your investment: it removes the grime, salt, and organic growth that break surfaces down over time, helps prevent slips on walkways, and keeps your home or business looking its best for customers and guests.

Is pressure washing safe for all surfaces?

Yes — when the right method is used. For delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, stucco, and shingles we use soft washing (low pressure plus safe cleaning solutions), and we reserve higher pressure for durable surfaces like concrete and pavers. The result is a deep clean without damage.

How much does pressure washing cost in Alberta?

Every property is different, so we always provide a fair, upfront quote before we begin — with no hidden fees. You'll know exactly what you owe before you commit to anything. Call 780-680-3753 or request a free quote online.

When is Alberta Pressure Washing open for business?

Our season runs through spring, summer, and fall, once temperatures stay reliably above freezing. Spring is our busiest time as homeowners wash off a winter's worth of salt and grime, so we recommend booking early to secure your spot.
