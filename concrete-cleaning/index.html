Concrete Cleaning Near Me in Alberta

If you need professional concrete cleaning services in Alberta, you’ve come to the right place! We are Alberta's Pressure Washing, a locally owned business that specializes in all things pressure washing. Of course, this includes concrete pressure washing, so if you’ve got any sort of dirty concrete surface, we want to make it clean! Some examples of these concrete surfaces include parking lots, sidewalks, driveways, patios, and even gigantic parking garages. (As you can see, there’s no job that’s too big or small for us!) But how do we actually do concrete washing? Great question! Every job starts with a routine chemical pre-treatment. To clarify, we apply sodium hypochlorite to the entire concrete surface, which immediately begins breaking down any present grime. Then, we’ll spray the area with pressurized water, swiftly removing that filth for good. Ta-da! Just like that, we’ve turned once-dirty concrete into a sparkling-clean masterpiece!

One of the best things about our concrete cleaning is how fast it is. In fact, our team members are all committed to your satisfaction, and a key component to your satisfaction is how much time you save. Don’t worry, as we won’t keep you impatiently waiting. To give you an idea of how quickly we work, a typical driveway wash takes us an hour or two to complete. We don’t achieve these record times by “cutting corners”, either. We work fast and thoroughly to ensure you leave us a five-star review! Does this sound like something you want to experience for yourself? If so, don’t wait another minute to contact us online. We want to hear from you!

Concrete Cleaning Services: Hoses vs. Surface Cleaners

During concrete cleaning services, there are two different tools we can use to remove grime from your surfaces. The first is our hoses. The second is our surface cleaners. So, the question is, what determines which tool we use during your project? It mostly depends on the area itself. For example, if your concrete is flat and horizontal—such as a sidewalk or driveway—our surface cleaners are the best choice for the job. However, if we’re working with vertical concrete walls, it’ll be better for us to hose them down. No matter what, though, expect us to work hard to remove 100% of that dirt and debris. That way, you’ll have zero complaints at the end of your appointment!
